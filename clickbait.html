About ClickGuard AI

ClickGuard AI is an end-to-end machine learning platform developed to identify sensationalized headline patterns, evaluate classification robustness against adversarial noise, and provide interpretable explanations for every decision.

Built with a TF-IDF Vectorizer (ngram range 1-2) and a high-accuracy Logistic Regression classifier trained on over 30,000 annotated news headlines.

Academic Context: Major Project Viva Demonstration & Benchmark Analysis.
